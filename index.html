About Me

My vocation is sharing Christ through art, fitness, and health education. My avocation is community outreach and honest humor.

I was born in Harlem, New York, and raised between the Bronx and the U.S. Virgin Islands. My passion for holistic wellness began early, inspired by my grandmother — a self-taught herbalist who modeled natural healing, faith, and service in everyday living.

I earned a Bachelor of Arts in Dance from the University of Maryland and enjoyed a career as a professional touring performer before moving into wellness education and ministry. That background in movement, theater, and storytelling still shapes how I teach, serve, and connect with people today.

Now I serve as a fitness coach with 1199SEIU Funds, specializing in Pilates, yoga, ballet conditioning, and strength and endurance training. I also provide wellness education through K2K Fit, serve as a Digital Chaplain with Adventist Connect, and am completing an internship with the Adventist Medical Evangelism Network. This ministry and outreach work has taken me to communities across North America, the Caribbean, and Latin America.

At Andrews University I'm pursuing Master of Social Work and Master of Divinity degrees, exploring how faith, public health, and community engagement work together to promote healing and human flourishing. Drawing on dance, theater, and comedy, I bring warmth, authenticity, and humor to every audience I serve.

My ministry is grounded in the belief that God cares about the health of our bodies, the peace of our minds, and the transformation of our hearts.

Journey

From the fitness studio to the seminary classroom — every season taught me a new way to care for people.

  1. Movement roots — University of Maryland

    A bachelor's degree in dance, followed by a career as a professional touring performer. Before anything else, I learned to communicate, heal, and worship through the moving body — studying the integrative fusion of literacy and movement, and how art influences society.

  2. New York Sports Clubs — Fitness Manager & Master Trainer

    Leading fitness operations with creative, strategic sales that surpassed income goals — while developing trainers and coaching clients. People came to move, but they stayed to talk. Annual community engagement events encouraged compassion and connection, and the studio became a community.

  3. 1199SEIU — Health & Wellness Educator

    Wellness education and movement programming for healthcare workers — Pilates, mobility, stress management, and practical self-care for the people who care for everyone else.

  4. Andrews University — seminary & social work

    An MDiv and MSW pursued together, because I refuse to choose between caring for someone's spirit and caring for their circumstances. Along the way: WOW Wednesdays wellness for seminarians, and brand and project leadership for the Graduate Student Association.

  5. NASW-Michigan Student Ambassador

    Selected to represent Andrews University's social work program as the liaison to the National Association of Social Workers, Michigan Chapter — promoting member resources and advocacy efforts, connecting students with the profession statewide, and planning events for social work students each semester.

  6. Pastoral ministry — Berean SDA Church

    Creative Arts Ministry Pastor, then Community Engagement Pastor — Sabbath outreach in shelters and memory care communities, worship at housing complexes, and wellness partnerships that took ministry beyond the church walls.

  7. Digital chaplaincy — Adventist Connect (AIM)

    Pastoral care for people who reach out online in vulnerable moments — turning digital contacts into real relationships through prayer, Scripture, and compassionate listening.

  8. AMEN — Social Work Intern, Community Health & Resource Navigation

    Serving free clinic guests with the Adventist Medical Evangelism Network — developing community and healthcare resource navigation sheets tailored to each clinic location, helping people navigate food, housing, transportation, and healthcare access, and assisting churches in exploring methods of maintaining and growing relationships after spiritual care follow-up, so a one-day clinic becomes a lasting connection.

  9. WAUS 90.7 FM — Voice Over Artist & Radio Announcer

    On-air support and voice work for Andrews University's classical station, serving Berrien Springs and South Bend — announcements, program introductions, and Michiana Classics broadcast breaks, delivered with warmth, clarity, and connection to the listener.

  10. SHINE — what's being built now

    A wellness collective and community empowerment hub where movement meets meaning — restoring hope, rebuilding health, and renewing community.

My Story

I started out helping people get strong. In studios across New York, I taught them to breathe, to stabilize, to move with intention. But class after class, something else kept happening — people lingered. They wanted to talk. About their stress, their families, their faith, their grief.

People came to me to train their bodies. They stayed because their souls needed a safe place.

That's when I understood my calling wasn't fitness alone. It was the whole person. So I went back to school — for ministry and for social work at the same time — because I refuse to choose between caring for someone's spirit and caring for their circumstances.

Today I move between the clinic, the classroom, the sanctuary, and the screen. I teach, I pray, I make people laugh, and I sit with them in the hard conversations. Everything I build — every class, every retreat, every circle — exists so that someone who has been carrying too much can finally set it down and begin again.
